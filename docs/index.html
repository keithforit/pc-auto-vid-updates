pc-auto-vid

Create short-form videos on your own PC, then share them straight to social.

What it does

pc-auto-vid is a desktop application that runs locally on your own computer. It helps you assemble narrated short-form videos from scenes, captions, music and stock footage, then optionally publish the finished video to your connected social accounts.

Social account connections

You can connect a TikTok account so that videos you create can be posted to your own profile. The connection uses TikTok's official, secure login. pc-auto-vid never sees your TikTok password, and your access tokens are stored only on your own computer — never on any external server.

  • We request only the permissions needed to identify your account and upload your videos.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings.
  • You are always in control of what gets posted.
