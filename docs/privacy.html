Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how pc-auto-vid ("the app", "we") handles your information. pc-auto-vid is a desktop application that runs locally on your own computer. We do not operate a central server that collects or stores your personal data.

1. Information the app handles

When you choose to connect a social account (such as TikTok), the app handles:

  • Basic profile information — your display name and account identifier, used only to show you which account is connected.
  • Authorization tokens — the secure access and refresh tokens returned by the social platform after you log in.

The app does not receive or store your social account password.

2. Where your data is stored

All connection details and tokens are stored locally on your own computer, in the app's configuration files (for example SocialConnections.json). This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party other than the social platform you are connecting to.

3. How your data is used

The information above is used solely to:

  • Display which account is currently connected.
  • Upload and publish videos that you choose to post, to your own account.
  • Refresh your authorization so you don't have to log in repeatedly.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Third-party platforms

When you connect TikTok or any other platform, your use of that platform is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. We only request the minimum permissions required to identify your account and upload your videos. For TikTok, see the TikTok Privacy Policy.

5. Removing your data

You can disconnect a social account at any time from the app's Settings, which removes the stored tokens from your computer. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age required by the connected platforms, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
